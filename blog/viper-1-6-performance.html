Viper — Performance & UX Pass (In Progress)

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • The instant launch described here shipped in 1.5.12 (see Two Weeks, Four Releases). There was no separate 1.6 release; versions continued as 1.5.x.

I recently sat down with people and watched them actually use Viper. Even I was surprised by the small things that broke the flow.

Why Now

At the start, I tested Viper on a single-bar connection and it was livable. But 20+ updates in, the codebase has grown enough that not every edge case is caught — and small frictions add up.

App Bootstrap

The annoying loading screen is gone. Chats now show up instantly when you open the app — no splash, no spinner while everything initializes.

What I'm Working On

Right now the focus is overall speed:

  • Account creation — faster, less waiting.
  • Account switching — snappier transitions between accounts.
  • General responsiveness — the small delays and rough edges I saw during testing.

On Testing on Bad Connections

I always test on the worst connection I can find. If it works there, it works everywhere. The app got a little heavier after 20 updates; this pass is about clearing that back up.

More soon — I'll post updates as the speedups land.
