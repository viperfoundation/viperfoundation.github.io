Viper 1.2: private reactions

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • Reactions are still end-to-end encrypted. Hold a message → Reactions; one each, a new one replaces yours.

Viper 1.2 is out.

What's new

Reactions.

Not the kind you're used to. In most messengers — WhatsApp, iMessage — reactions are visible to the server. The platform knows what you reacted to, when, and with what emoji. That metadata is part of your profile.

Our big brother Telegram (we are a derivative messenger from Telegram because we are based on The Open Network) stores reactions on their servers too.

In Viper 1.2, reactions are end-to-end encrypted. The same cryptographic guarantees that protect your messages protect your reactions. The server sees only encrypted content.

Why it matters

Reactions feel small. They're not.

A reaction is a signal. It tells someone — and the platform — that a specific message affected you in a specific way. Over time, that's a behavioral fingerprint.

We encrypted reactions for the same reason we encrypted messages: because your responses to what you read are just as private as what you write.
