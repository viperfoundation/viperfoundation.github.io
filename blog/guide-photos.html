Photos and videos

Send, open, save, and how big they can be

Three ways in

Tap the paperclip. Photo or Video picks up to 10; each goes as its own message, in the order you picked. Camera takes a photo, on iPhone only. Paste Image sends one you copied.

Sent as they are

A picked photo or video goes as the original file: not resized, not re-encoded. A camera shot or a pasted image goes as JPEG. There is no caption: send the text as a message.

Open and save

A photo shows as itself: tap it for full size, then Share → Save Image. A video shows its first frame and length; tap it to play full screen.

All in one place

Tap the name at the top of the chat. Under Shared in this chat, Media holds every photo and video; tap one to open it, with Share.

How big

They travel like any file: see Files. Direct: no size limit, free, while you are both online. Via relay: 50 MB free, 2 GB with Viper Pro. Bluetooth: up to 8 MB, 1 GB with Wi-Fi; bigger goes via relay.
