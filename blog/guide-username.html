@username and TON Domains

A name on the relay, or on the blockchain

A name on the relay

A @username is a short name for a public key. The relay keeps the list and answers Search, so you trust the relay: it could point a name at another key.

TON Domains

A .ton domain is a name on the TON blockchain, held by a wallet. Type it in Search with .ton: the relay looks up who holds it on-chain.

Check it yourself

Turn on Show TON Domains in Settings → Appearance. A profile then lists the .ton names held by wallets of that key, from TON and tonapi.io, not the relay.

What it proves

A name listed there sits on a wallet of that very key, and a relay cannot change that. It shows only when the wallet comes from the same key.
