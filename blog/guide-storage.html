Storage

What takes space on this device and what you can delete

Where it is

Settings → Security and Data → Storage: what Viper keeps on this device. Messages, media and files, AI chats and AI models, each with its size.

Delete a chat

Chats are listed biggest first. Swipe one and tap Delete: its messages go, with the files only it uses. A photo another chat still shows is kept.

What stays

It is gone from this device only: nothing is sent to them. The chat leaves Chats, a contact stays in Contacts. Saved Messages cannot be deleted here.

AI chats and models

A model takes gigabytes: Edge0 8B is 4.58 GB. Swipe it to get the space back; AI Chat then offers Download again. AI chats go one by one, or with Delete all AI chats.
