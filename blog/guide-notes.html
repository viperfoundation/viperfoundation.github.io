Notes

A note in the chat that you can change later

Write a note

In a chat, tap the paperclip, then Note. Add a title, the text, or both, and tap Send: it arrives as a card.

Edit it later

Hold your note and tap Edit. Edit Note opens with the title and the text; change either and tap Save.

On both phones

Your edit replaces the note on their phone too, marked edited. It does not arrive as a new message.

Only the author

Only the person who wrote a note can edit it, and a forwarded note cannot be edited. Their notes you can reply to, copy or forward.

A message like any other

A note and each edit go end-to-end encrypted over the relay, a direct link or Bluetooth, like a message. The text holds up to 4,000 characters.
