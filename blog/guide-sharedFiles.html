Shared Files

Files your contacts download straight from your phone

Files on your profile

Profile → Shared Files, at the bottom: tap + and pick a file. Viper keeps its own copy and lists it with its size.

Who sees them

They tap your name at the top of a chat, then Browse shared files. The list comes over a direct link, so you must have each other in contacts. They see every file on it.

Phone to phone

They tap the arrow and the file comes straight from your phone, piece by piece, each checked by SHA-256. No relay, no size limit, no Pro.

Stay online

The file comes from your phone, not the relay: they can download it only while you are online. Under each file you see who is downloading it and how far, then done.

Removing a file

Tap the bin: Viper deletes its copy, and a download in progress fails. Your original stays where it was. The list survives a restart, kept per account.
