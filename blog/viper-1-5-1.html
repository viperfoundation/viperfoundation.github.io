Viper 1.5.1 — P2P Button in All Chats

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • The bolt is in every chat, but a direct link now needs both of you to have each other in contacts (since 1.5.16).

A small but meaningful change: the P2P Bolt button is now shown in all chats — not just conversations with saved contacts.

Previously, you could only initiate a direct P2P connection with someone in your contact list. Now any open conversation has the Bolt button, so you can go direct with anyone — contacts, search results, or someone who recently messaged you.
