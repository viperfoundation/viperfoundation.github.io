Reputation

What the score counts and what it does not prove

Counted by the relay

Your Profile and people's profiles show a Reputation ring. The relay counts it from what it can check itself. Tap it to see each signal.

Four signals

A @username +10. Account age: 30, 90 or 365 days give +5, +10 or +20. A .ton domain on this key +40. Viper Pro +30. At most 100.

Four levels

New above 0, Known from 20, Established from 40, Verified from 70. Verified means a high score, not a checked identity.

What it does not prove

Nobody rates anyone, and nothing you write counts. It does not say who the person is or that they can be trusted. It is what your relay reports.
