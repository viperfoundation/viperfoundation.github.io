Calls

Audio and video, end-to-end encrypted, through the relay

Call from a chat

In a chat, tap the phone at the top for a call or the camera for a video call. Calls are free. If their Viper is too old for calls, you see Calls unavailable.

Audio or video

A video call rings with Decline, Audio and Video: Audio answers with your camera off. During a call, Camera turns yours on or off and Flip switches it.

How it rings

On iPhone the system call screen rings while Viper is running. If Viper is closed, a notification comes first: open Viper in time and it rings. On a Mac, macOS gives Viper no microphone or camera yet.

Through the relay, sealed

Voice and video always go through the relay, never direct. Each call gets its own new key: end-to-end encrypted, the relay passes on what it cannot read.

Missed calls

A call rings for 45 seconds. Then you see No answer and they get a Missed call, also if they were offline and come back within a day. Every call leaves a line in the chat.
