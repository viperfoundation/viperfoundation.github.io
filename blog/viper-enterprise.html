Viper Enterprise — Private Messaging Infrastructure for Organizations

Note

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • Not re-checked: this post describes an offering for organizations, not features of the App Store app. The App Store app lets you change the relay key in Settings → Advanced.

Most enterprise messaging tools require employees to register with a phone number or corporate email. That means your communication infrastructure is tied to identifiers you don't fully control — and that can be taken away, spoofed, or leaked.

Viper Enterprise is built on a different premise: own your messenger, own your infrastructure, own your identity.

No Phone Numbers. No Email Addresses.

Employee identities in Viper are based on cryptographic keypairs — specifically Ed25519 keys, the same primitive used by the TON network. There are no phone numbers or email addresses involved. This eliminates entire classes of attacks: SIM swapping, email takeover, credential stuffing.

An employee's identity is a key. Nothing more.

Mnemonic Phrase & Private Key Auth

Accounts are portable and fully owned by the employee. Each account can be backed up with a 24-word mnemonic phrase, and imported on any device using a private key. The employee holds the key — not the organization, not the vendor.

Self-Hosted or Private Deployment

Viper runs on a relay architecture. For enterprise deployments, you bring your own relay — installed on your own servers or private cloud. Messages never touch infrastructure you don't control.

We support fully air-gapped deployments for environments where no public internet involvement is acceptable.

Private Network Topology

Define your own network topology — control how nodes connect, route messages across internal segments, and isolate departments or teams within a fully private overlay network. No public relay involvement required.

Fully Customizable Relay

Relay routing, security policies, and connection rules are fully configurable. You're not locked into a fixed architecture — the relay can be shaped to fit your existing network and compliance requirements.

Open Integration Architecture

Viper Enterprise supports custom authentication backends, internal API integrations, and enterprise system hooks. There's no walled garden — if you need to connect it to your identity provider, HR system, or access control layer, that's possible.

Enterprise Branding

The client can be white-labeled with your company's logo, colors, and identity. Employees see your brand, not ours.

Low Infrastructure Cost

The relay architecture is lightweight by design. Running Viper infrastructure for hundreds or thousands of employees doesn't require significant compute — the model is built to keep operational costs low at scale.

Deployment Assistance & Consulting

We work directly with your team through the entire deployment process — relay setup, network configuration, identity provisioning, and integration with existing workflows. You're not handed documentation and left alone.

Built on The Open Network

Viper's cryptographic foundation is built on TON networking primitives — modern, auditable, and open. The identity model is transparent and doesn't depend on proprietary black boxes.
