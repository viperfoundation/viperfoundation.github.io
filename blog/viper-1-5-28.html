Viper 1.5.28

Calls, profiles and a lot more in your chats.

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • Calls always go through the relay, end-to-end encrypted with a new key per call. On a Mac, macOS gives Viper no microphone or camera yet.
  • Your profile name and photo are public: anyone who has your key can see them.
  • Audio and video calls — one-to-one, end-to-end encrypted. Calls connect reliably and the voice stays clear, even on a weak connection.
  • Profile name and photo — set a nickname and a picture; tap someone's photo to see it full size.
  • Live location — share where you are for 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours. Shared locations open on a map right inside Viper.
  • Edit your messages, and react to any message — over the relay, a direct connection or Bluetooth.
  • Reply, forward, send photos from the camera, swipe to reply, search inside a chat, and see shared media and links in a contact's profile.
  • Voice messages can be transcribed on your device — free, nothing leaves your phone.
  • Save video circles to your Photos.

Steadier everywhere:

  • Viper stays reachable when it is in the background, and comes back faster.
  • Works better on one bar of signal — messages and presence keep flowing.
  • Direct connections are easier to set up and no longer drop on their own.
