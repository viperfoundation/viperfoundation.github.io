Viper 1.5.18 — Hide Your Online Status

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • Hide Online Status is still Viper Pro: you show as offline to everyone.
  • Anon Mode is described in the app today as: it takes you out of Search, and your chats keep working. The relay checks Apple's signature itself.
  • Hide Typing and read receipts are free switches in Settings → Privacy.

Viper Pro is fully live. The last piece of the subscription launch — payments — landed in 1.5.17. 1.5.18 closes the privacy half: Pro users can now hide their online status.

What's New

  • Hide your online status (Pro) — your presence dot no longer broadcasts. Other users see you as offline even when you're in the app. Pairs with Anonymous Mode from 1.5.16 for a fully invisible profile.
  • Viper Pro is fully available — subscriptions are live for everyone, no review hold, no gating.

Where It Goes Next

With Pro shipped and stable, the focus moves to what Viper becomes on top of a working privacy substrate. The next releases lean into transport — making the network you talk over something you actually choose, not something the app guesses for you.
