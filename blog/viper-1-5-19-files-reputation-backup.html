Viper 1.5.19 — Offline File Delivery, Reputation & Encrypted Backups

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • Relay files: up to 50 MB free, 2 GB with Viper Pro, as before. A file waits for the recipient up to 14 days; once delivered, the relay deletes it.
  • Encrypted Chat Backup is no longer Pro-only: messages, contacts and Encrypted Notes go into the backup for free; Pro adds photos, videos, files, voice and video messages. Import is free.
  • Hide Typing and Send Read Receipts are free now (they were Pro as “Extended Invisibility”). Settings → Privacy.
  • Reputation now has fixed points: @username +10, account age +5/+10/+20, a .ton domain on this key +40, Viper Pro +30, at most 100. See Reputation.

The biggest Viper update since the Pro launch. Large files that reach people even when they're offline — without giving up an inch of privacy. A smarter way to know who you're talking to. Encrypted backups. And finer control over what others see.

Send Files Without Being Online Together

  • Share files up to 50 MB in any chat — delivered even when the recipient is offline.
  • End-to-end encrypted. The relay deletes every file the moment it's delivered. Nothing lingers.
  • Viper Pro raises the limit to 2 GB.
  • File transfers now run on a faster, more reliable transport.

Reputation

Profiles now show a trust score built from verifiable signals:

  • Username
  • Account age
  • A verified .ton domain
  • Pro support

Computed by the relay. Nothing about you is shared beyond what's already public.

Encrypted Chat Backup (Pro)

Export your message history as an encrypted file keyed to your account. Restore it on a fresh install and your chats are back.

Typing & Read Receipts

  • See when someone is typing and when your message was read.
  • Extended Invisibility (Pro) — turn off your typing indicator and read receipts.

Also

  • The messages from non-contacts toggle is now free for everyone.
