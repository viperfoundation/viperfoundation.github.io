Enter via Face ID

A lock on Viper, and what it does not do

Turn it on

Settings → Security and Data → Enter via Face ID. It is off by default; turning it on asks for Face ID first.

When it asks

At launch, before Viper connects or loads your chats, and every time you come back from the background. Sharing to Viper from another app asks too.

Face ID or passcode

If Face ID does not match, your device passcode opens Viper too. Cancelled? The lock stays: tap Enter via Face ID to try again.

Your keys ask on their own

Show on the Account Key or the Recovery Phrase asks for Face ID every time, with this switch on or off. Hide puts them away.

A lock, not a cipher

It locks the door and encrypts nothing more. Your chats are encrypted on this phone either way, with a key made from your Account Key. One switch covers every account here.
