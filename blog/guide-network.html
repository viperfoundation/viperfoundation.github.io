Network Diagnostics

Your network type and what it means for direct links

Where it is

Settings → Help → Network Diagnostics. Your network type, your addresses and every change, live.

Network type

Viper compares the addresses on this device with the one the relay sees: No NAT, NAT behind your router, or CGNAT, an address your carrier shares.

P2P or relay

The badge follows the mapping test, not the type. Cone: hole punching works, direct P2P is expected. Symmetric: chats go through the relay, and Viper still tries a direct link.

Always on, sends nothing

While Viper runs, it notes every network switch, IP and NAT change, relay and direct link, and background stay. It only reads: it sends nothing.

Share the log

Something does not work? Tap the share button at the top: a text file with the journal. It holds your IP addresses; message text never gets in.
