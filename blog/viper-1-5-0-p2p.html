Viper 1.5 — Experimental P2P Mode

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • P2P is no longer “experimental” in the app: a direct link starts with Route → Connect and comes back by itself until you tap Disconnect.
  • Both P2P and the relay run over QUIC since 1.5.14–1.5.15. By default the relay goes first; Settings → Connectivity changes the order.
  • Direct needs both of you in each other's contacts.

Viper 1.5 ships with experimental P2P mode — the first step toward relay-free messaging.

What's new

  • Real peer-to-peer messaging with direct device-to-device communication.
  • Relay bootstrap discovery — devices find each other through the Viper relay bootstrap node, then communicate directly.
  • Bolt component — use the Bolt component in any chat to coordinate a direct P2P connection with the other side.

Reliability note

P2P delivery is currently less reliable than relay-based delivery. It works well under ideal conditions — a fresh connection without backgrounding. We're treating this as experimental and will be improving stability in subsequent releases.

This is the foundation. 1.5.x updates build directly on top of it.
