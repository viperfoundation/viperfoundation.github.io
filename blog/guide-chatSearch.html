Search in chat

Find a message by its words, on this phone

Open the search

In a chat, tap the magnifying glass at the top and type. A list shows every match, newest first, with its date. With a keyboard, ⌘F opens it too.

What it finds

The text, the quote in a reply, a file's name, an event's title and place, a note's title. Capitals and accents do not matter. Voice and video messages are skipped.

Step through

The arrows or Return go from one match to the next, or tap one in the list. The chat scrolls to it and the message flashes.

On this phone only

Search reads the whole chat stored on this phone, not only what is on screen. Nothing is sent to the relay. Done closes it.
