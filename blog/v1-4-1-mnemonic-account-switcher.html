Release notes · June 6, 2026 · Updated for Viper 1.5.34

v1.4.1 — Mnemonic Phrase, Account Switcher & Onboarding

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • Today: Profile → Accounts → Generate New Account. From Recovery Phrase gives 24 words, and the Account Key and your TON wallet are made from them. From Account Key makes a random key with no phrase.
  • Import Account takes a 64-character Account Key or a Recovery Phrase. See Your keys and Several accounts.

Viper 1.4.1 is a focused quality-of-life update built on top of the TON Address feature introduced in 1.3.

Background

In v1.3 we shipped TON Address display — your on-chain address derived from your Viper key and rendered in the profile screen. At the time, the derivation was handled by an external script.

What's New in 1.4.1

Mnemonic Phrase Generation

You can now generate a mnemonic phrase directly inside Viper to activate your TON Address. No external tools required — tap Generate in the TON Address section, and the app produces a 24-word seed phrase tied to your key.

Store it somewhere safe. The phrase is the only way to recover your TON Address on another device.

Improved Account Switcher

The account switching screen has been redesigned. Switching between identities is now faster and clearer, with a dedicated screen that shows all your accounts at a glance.

Improved Onboarding Screen

First-run experience has been polished. The onboarding screen now guides new users through the key concepts — identity, encryption, and TON Address — with cleaner layout and copy.
