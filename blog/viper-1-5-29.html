Viper 1.5.29

P2P improvements

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • Route order today: relay first by default, a Bluetooth link still wins; change it in Settings → Connectivity. The 1.5.29 note lists Bluetooth, direct, relay.
  • Our first draft of these notes (not the published App Store text) also listed: Bluetooth asks first with an Allow/Decline card, the line under the name shows the current route, and all connection settings moved to Settings → Connectivity.
  • Talk directly. Messages go straight to your friend's phone, at home or on mobile data, even with one bar of signal.

Also new

  • Router. Viper picks the way for every message: Bluetooth when you are close, a direct link, or the relay. If one drops, it switches on its own. Set the order in Settings. Much better UX when choosing a route for a message.
  • Viper Bluetooth Radar: see which way people nearby are and how far.
  • Calendar events and notes, right in the chat.
  • Share to Viper from other apps.
  • Plus many fixes.
