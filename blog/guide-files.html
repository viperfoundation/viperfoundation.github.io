Files

Sending a document, and the three ways it travels

Send a file

In a chat, tap the paperclip, then File; or tap Share in another app and pick Viper. It goes the way the line under the name shows: relay first by default.

Direct: no limit, free

With Route priority on Direct and a link made with Connect, a file goes phone to phone: no size limit, no Pro, you both just need to be online. Their phone checks its SHA-256.

Relay: 50 MB or 2 GB

Via relay it is encrypted for them first: the relay sees the size, never the name or content. Up to 50 MB free, up to 2 GB with Viper Pro.

It waits for them

They need not be online when you send. Their phone fetches the file by itself and, after a dropped connection or a relaunch, carries on where it stopped. Then the relay deletes it.

Bluetooth: no internet

Nearby, a file of up to 8 MB goes over Bluetooth, up to 1 GB when Wi-Fi can join in. A dropped link resumes where it stopped; a bigger file takes the relay.
