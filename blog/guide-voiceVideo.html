Voice and video messages

Hold to record, tap for a video circle

Hold to record

With the message field empty, the button on the right records. Hold it and speak; let go to send, or slide left to cancel.

Tap for a circle

A quick tap switches that button between voice and a video circle. Hold to record a circle of up to 30 seconds; a second finger flips the camera.

The way messages go

A note takes the route the line under the name shows, end-to-end encrypted, like a file. Via relay it waits for someone offline; if Bluetooth or direct fails, the relay takes it.

One after another

Tap a note to play it; the next one in the chat starts by itself. The bar at the top pauses it and sets 1X, 1.5X or 2X.

Text and Photos

Hold a voice message and tap Transcribe: Russian speech turns into text on this phone, free. Hold a circle and tap Save Video; Save without Logo is Viper Pro.
