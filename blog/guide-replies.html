Message menu

Reply, edit, react, forward, transcribe, select

Hold a message

Hold any message to open its menu. Edit is there only on a text message you wrote: not on theirs, a forward, a photo or a file.

Reply to a message

Tap Reply, or swipe the message to the left, and write. Your answer carries a quote of it; tap the quote to jump to the original.

Fix what you wrote

Tap Edit: the text is back in the field, under Editing. Send it, and it changes on both phones, marked edited. No new message arrives.

Add a reaction

Tap Reactions and pick an emoji: it shows under the message on both phones, encrypted like a message. One each: a new one replaces yours.

Forward it

Tap Forward and pick a chat, Saved Messages first. Text arrives marked Forwarded from, with the first author's name; a photo, file or voice message goes as a new one.

Voice into text

Hold a voice message and tap Transcribe: the text opens with Copy. A Russian speech model inside the app does it, with no download and no internet, and the text is not saved.

Select and delete

Select up to this message picks it and every newer one; tap others to add or drop them. ✨ takes them to the AI (see the AI guide). Delete for me removes them from this phone only.
