Release notes · November 7, 2026 · Updated for Viper 1.5.34

Viper 1.5.33

Encrypted Notes, Viper Pro on every account, and keys you can copy.

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • 1.5.32 (build 335) was submitted on 2026-10-28 with these same notes but is not in the App Store version history; they shipped as 1.5.33.
  • Encrypted Notes: Profile → Encrypted Notes. Your own notes, kept only on this device and encrypted with your account key. Send any note to a chat as a note card.
  • The encrypted chat backup now carries your notes. Messages, contacts and notes are free; with Viper Pro the backup also keeps photos, videos, files, voice and video messages.
  • Viper Pro works on the account that subscribed. Settings → Subscription shows which account has it.
  • Pro on your other accounts: any other account can buy Viper Pro for itself for one month, from the same Apple ID, as many times as you need. It does not renew.
  • Profiles show a person's public key and reputation. Copying a public key now says Copied.
  • Hide typing and read receipts are free for everyone.
  • Reputation updates right after a purchase.
  • Profile rows have icons, and the Viper Pro guide explains accounts and Apple IDs.
