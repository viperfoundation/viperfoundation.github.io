Viper Guide — July 2026

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • Files are no longer P2P-only. Through the relay: up to 50 MB free, 2 GB with Pro, even when the other person is offline (kept up to 14 days, deleted once delivered). Direct: no size limit. Bluetooth: up to 8 MB (1 GB with Wi-Fi).
  • Direct P2P now opens only when you have each other in contacts and someone taps Connect in Route; by default the relay goes first.
  • Contacts: the authoritative list is on your device, encrypted. The app also still sends each contact you add to the relay (your key → their key, the name encrypted to you).
  • Shared Files: the list comes over a direct link, so you must have each other in contacts.
  • .ton names in profiles show only with Settings → Appearance → Show TON Domains, read from TON and tonapi.io, not the relay.
  • The old screenshots were replaced with Viper 1.5.34 screens.

A quick tour of what Viper is right now, and the ideas behind it. It's a Frankenstein mix of Telegram, VKontakte, and torrents — your data, on your device.

Contacts & Chats — Everything on Your Device

Contacts and chats live entirely on your device. I keep almost nothing on the server.

Your Account Is Your Wallet

If you created your account through a mnemonic phrase, your Viper account is a TON wallet. Open any TON wallet app, enter the same phrase, and you'll see the exact same wallet.

Your Recovery Phrase is 24 words — a human-readable way to remember the private key without actually memorizing a long string. You can also derive a wallet from a private key directly, but that's painful and probably not worth it.

Want to derive the private key from those 24 words yourself, outside of Viper? See the Generating a Wallet with tonutils-go writeup — same 24 words, same wallet, no Viper required.

NFT Usernames — Trustless Identity

You don't need to trust my server. If someone has an NFT username (like debugname111.ton), you can pull their public key straight from the blockchain — that's 100% guaranteed to be that person.

P2P Messages & File Transfers

When the other person is online, you talk peer-to-peer — directly, no relay involved.

Files are P2P-only for now.

Shared Files

Going to an event and need to hand out a presentation? Upload a file to your profile. Others can download it — but only while you're online.

This frees up server bandwidth — no 24/7 file hosting on my end.

Closing

Viper ended up a strange mix: Telegram's chat model, VKontakte's profile and file sharing, and torrents' peer-to-peer distribution. A Frankenstein. Your data, your keys, your rules.
