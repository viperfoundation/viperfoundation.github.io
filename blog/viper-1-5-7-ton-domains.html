Viper 1.5.7 — TON Domains NFT Support

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • Type a name with .ton in Search: the relay looks up who holds it on-chain.
  • In profiles, .ton names held by wallets of that key show only with Settings → Appearance → Show TON Domains, read from TON and tonapi.io.
  • The relay key is in Settings → Advanced.

Viper 1.5.7 integrates TON Domains — the NFT-based naming system on The Open Network.

What's new

TON Domains NFT Support

Users can now be discovered via their .ton domain name. When someone owns a TON Domain NFT, their Viper identity can be resolved through it — search for name.ton and find the corresponding user directly.

Resolution uses TON liteservers, keeping the lookup decentralized and trustless.

TON Domains in Profile

If a contact has a .ton domain linked to their identity, it now appears in their profile via TON API. It's another layer of verifiable identity — alongside their public key, you can see the domain name they own on-chain.

Relay Key Management Improvements

This release also improves relay key management in Settings, making it easier to configure and rotate relay credentials.

TON Domains support is a meaningful step toward human-readable, blockchain-verified identities in Viper — names you own, not usernames assigned by a platform.
