Notifications

What a push says, and what stays quiet

What a push says

When Viper is closed or iOS has paused it, the relay sends a push through Apple. It says only Viper and New message: no text, no name, no key.

Tap to open the chat

The push holds only a short hash of the sender. Your phone matches it against your chats and opens the right one. From one person, a new push replaces the last.

Quiet on purpose

Read marks, reactions, edits and Live Location updates go silent: the relay never pushes them. With Viper open, no banner covers the chat.

Bluetooth and calls

A message over Bluetooth gets a notification made on your phone, with the name and the text: the relay never saw it. A call to a closed Viper comes as New message first: open Viper in time and it rings.

Turning them off

Viper asks once, after your first connection. To change it: iOS Settings → Viper → Notifications. Exit, Delete Account and Erase All Data stop pushes for the account in use.
