Events

Send a plan, add it to the Calendar

Make an event

In a chat, tap the paperclip, then Event. A title and a start are enough; an end, a place and a note are up to you.

Your own time zone

It arrives as a card with the date and the time. Each of you sees the time in your own time zone; an all-day event is the same day for everyone.

Into your Calendar

Tap Add to Calendar under the card. Viper asks only to add events, never to read them. The editor opens filled in: pick a calendar and confirm.

A message like any other

An event goes end-to-end encrypted over the relay, a direct link or Bluetooth, like a message. Once sent it cannot be edited; Forward and Copy work.
