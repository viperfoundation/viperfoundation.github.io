Live Location

Where you are, for a while, to one chat

Share for a while

On iPhone, in a chat: tap the paperclip, then Live Location. Pick 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours. One share per chat at a time.

It follows you

Your pin moves as you do: at most every 15 seconds, and only after 20 m or more. It keeps going in the background, with the iOS location indicator.

What they see

A map in the chat: live until what time, and how fresh the pin is. When sharing ends, it says so. A tap opens it on a map, with directions.

Stop any time

Paperclip → Stop Live Location, or hold your map and tap Stop Sharing. Otherwise it stops by itself at the time you picked.

Only this chat

Your position travels inside the end-to-end encrypted chat, by the same route as a message. The relay never sees where you are. Updates come silently, with no notification.
