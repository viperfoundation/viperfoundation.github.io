Viper 1.5.3 — File Upload Improvements & P2P Speed Indicator

Viper 1.5.3 focuses on file transfer quality.

What's new

  • File uploading improvements — general reliability and performance improvements to the upload pipeline.
  • P2P upload speed indicator — when transferring files over a direct P2P connection, you can now see the current upload speed in real time.

The speed indicator gives you visibility into the P2P connection quality during a transfer, making it easier to understand what's happening on slower or variable connections.
