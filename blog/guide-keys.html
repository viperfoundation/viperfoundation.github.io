Your keys

Account Key, recovery phrase, public key

A pair of keys

Your account is a pair of keys. The public key is your address: show it to anyone. The Account Key is its private half: keep it to yourself.

The recovery phrase

Generate from Recovery Phrase gives 24 words, and the Account Key and your TON wallet are made from them. An account made or imported from a key has no phrase.

Whoever has them is you

With your Account Key or your phrase, anyone can sign in as you, get your messages and write as you. There is no reset: never send them to anyone.

Where they are

Settings → Security and Data. Tap Show: Face ID or the passcode comes first. Your public key stays on your Profile.
