Viper 1.5.5 — P2P with Any User

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • Changed: since 1.5.16 a direct P2P link opens only when each of you has the other in contacts. Otherwise the offer is refused and the chat stays on the relay.
  • The bolt now opens Route: Auto, Bluetooth for this person, or Relay only, plus Connect for a direct link. See How your messages travel.

Viper 1.5.5 expands who you can connect with over P2P.

What's new

The Bolt P2P component now works with:

  • Contacts — as before.
  • Users found through search — initiate a direct connection right from search results.
  • Users who recently messaged you — anyone in your recent conversations can be connected to via P2P directly.

This removes friction for establishing direct connections with new contacts before they're saved, and makes P2P a first-class option throughout the app.
