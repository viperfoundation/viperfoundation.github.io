v1.4 — Deeplinks

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • viper:// links still open in the app (viper://u/<name>); an @username in a message becomes such a link.
  • Not re-checked: the support and developer accounts named below.

Viper 1.4 ships deeplink support.

A deeplink is a URI that opens a specific destination inside the app directly — skipping the home screen and landing exactly where the link points.

The Scheme

All Viper deeplinks follow the pattern:

viper://<resource>/<identifier>

The first supported resource type is u — a user profile:

viper://u/<username>

Examples

LinkOpens
viper://u/supportSupport chat
viper://u/designervoidDeveloper profile
viper://u/<your-username>Your own profile

Why It Matters

Deeplinks make it possible to share Viper contacts the same way you'd share any URL — in a website, a README, a QR code, or a message in another app. The recipient taps the link and lands directly in the conversation.

This is the first step toward a richer linking model.

Reaching Support

You can now open the support chat directly:

viper://u/support

Tap it on a device with Viper installed, or copy it into any browser — the app will open and navigate to the support conversation.
