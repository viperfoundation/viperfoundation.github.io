Viper 1.5.11 — Video & Voice Messages, Apple Review Updates

Updated for Viper 1.5.34 (November 19, 2026)

What changed since this post. Where the post and the app differ, the app is right.

  • Voice: hold the record button; tap it to switch to a video circle of up to 30 seconds. Playback continues to the next note, at 1X, 1.5X or 2X.
  • Hold a voice message → Transcribe: Russian speech to text on the phone, free. Save Video on a circle; Save without Logo is Viper Pro.
  • Through the relay a voice or video note now waits for someone offline, like a file (it is deleted once delivered).

Viper 1.5.11 adds native video and voice messaging, and ships a few changes required to pass Apple's review.

Video & Voice Messages

You can now send video and voice messages. These work over both the relay and direct P2P — the app picks the best transport based on whether you and your contact are online at the same time.

  • Voice messages — record and send audio inline.
  • Video messages — record and send short video clips.

Like text, these are routed directly when both peers are online and never touch persistent storage on the server.

Removed: Open In

The "Open In" functionality has been removed from the app.

Removed: Wallet Address in Another User's Profile

Apple requires this feature to go through an organization-level flow, which isn't set up yet — so wallet address display has been removed from other people's profiles in this build.

As an alternative: you can copy a contact's public key from their profile and convert it to a wallet address yourself using tools outside of Viper. I'll write about whether this approach passes Apple review in a future update.
