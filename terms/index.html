Nature of the service

  • Viper is a private messenger. There is no sign-up form, phone number or email: an account is a cryptographic key pair created on your device. Online, the app connects to our relay with your public key; without internet it works over Bluetooth with people nearby.
  • Your Account Key and recovery phrase are generated on your device and controlled only by you. We do not store, manage or have access to them, and we cannot recover an account, messages or wallet funds if they are lost.
  • Messages are end-to-end encrypted. Our relay passes them on and deletes each one once it is delivered. Messages may also go directly between devices or over Bluetooth.
  • Some features depend on third parties: Apple (App Store, push notifications), the TON network, tonapi.io and Hugging Face (AI model downloads). Their availability is outside our control.

Messaging and encryption

We cannot read, monitor or moderate the content of your messages, because they are end-to-end encrypted. You can control who reaches you: turn off Messages from Non-Contacts in Settings → Privacy, and delete chats from your device.

On-device AI

AI Chat runs open models on your device. Answers can be wrong; check important details. The AI only drafts messages, events and notes: nothing is sent until you confirm. The models are provided by their authors under their own licenses.
